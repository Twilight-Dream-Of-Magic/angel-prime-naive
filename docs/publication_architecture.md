# Publication Architecture

The mathematical and engineering claims are now published in two coordinated
documents instead of one mixed manuscript.

## Mathematical annals article

`paper/Angel_Arithmetic_Executable_Naive_Prime_Program_Joint_Time_Space_2026-08-26.pdf`
contains the definitions, theorem statements, proofs, scoped complexity model,
and open problems. Its new opening part states the formal Angel maze object,
ten three-class axioms, all four high-dimensional operations, causal functors,
and deterministic class-quantum history algebra. It then connects the factorial
maze, cyclic closure and Volume V residual-horizon charts to the five-volume
Angel Prime program. Difficult proofs, source
provenance, and finite evidence are separated into labeled appendices. Build
transcripts, sanitizer status, hash manifests, or a release checklist are not
used as mathematical argument.

## Engineering companion

`paper/Angel_SDK_1.2.0_Engineering_Audit_2026-08-26.pdf` contains the
implementation map, finite differential campaigns, deterministic counter
tables, toolchain evidence, compatibility status, and known limitations. Those
items are evidence that this implementation conforms to the mathematics on the
tested domain; they are not substitutes for proofs.

The source archive retains raw logs under `results/` for exact reproduction.
The visual language is shared across both PDFs so the pair reads as one colored
annals release while keeping theorem and audit roles cleanly separated.

The integrated source supplied for Volumes I--IV is preserved byte-for-byte at
`paper/source_material/Angel_Prime_Mathematical_Annals_Integrated_Volumes_I_IV.tex`.
The provenance appendix maps its unitized history algebra, singular quotient,
causal histories, path packets, and cyclic theory to the normalized revision;
new executable claims are identified separately.

The separately supplied Volume V v5.9 strict research package is likewise
preserved byte-for-byte under `paper/source_material/`, together with selected
TeX, claim-boundary, traceability and verifier sources. Its residual-horizon
results are labelled verifier-only; they are not represented as public SDK
interfaces.

The structural theory is deliberately placed after the executable and Volume
V main lines. It proves the finite/pro-object statements, then reports the
4,961-record Wilson/cyclic/rank/jet campaign, and finally isolates all-chart
reconstruction, integral generalized HDIV and native birth as conjectural or
open. This keeps the algorithm narrative direct while allowing the larger
mathematics to be developed without being mistaken for shipped API behavior.
